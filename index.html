Open-Cell vs Closed-Cell

Open-Cell Spray Foam

Best for interior walls, attics, and sound control.

Main benefit: More affordable and great for air sealing.

Closed-Cell Spray Foam

Best for metal buildings, crawl spaces, moisture-prone areas.

Main benefit: Higher R-value, moisture resistance, and added strength.

Not sure which one you need? We will explain the difference during your free estimate.

How is Pricing Determined?

We don't believe in hidden fees. The cost of your project depends on several factors:

  • Size of the area (Square footage).
  • Foam type: open-cell or closed-cell.
  • Required thickness (R-value).
  • Access difficulty & Ventilation.
  • Existing insulation removal.
  • New construction versus retrofit.
